## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for rigorous, distraction-free educational environments, digital research portals, and dual-language university systems. It balances scholarly gravitas with contemporary digital precision. The target audience encompasses undergraduate and graduate students, faculty researchers, and institutional administrators who require sustained cognitive focus over extended sessions.

### Design Movement: Modern Academic Clarity
The design style blends contemporary institutional minimalism with crisp information density. It avoids superficial ornament, gamified distraction, and excessive motion, prioritizing:
- High typographic discipline with natural parity between Latin and Arabic scripts.
- Dense, highly structured content architectures suited for syllabi, reading lists, grade books, and analytical dashboards.
- Clear structural boundaries, predictable layout shifts across bidirectional (LTR/RTL) contexts, and dependable affordances.

## Colors

The palette is engineered to meet strict WCAG 2.1 AAA contrast targets for long-form reading and analytical tasks, using deep, authoritative pigments against soft, paper-like surfaces.

- **Primary (`#1E3A8A`)**: Deep Academic Indigo. Serves as the principal brand signifier, key structural headers, interactive elements, and primary callouts. Yields > 10:1 contrast against pure white and off-white canvas surfaces.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7`)**: Electric Sky. Applied for secondary actions, interactive focus states, active tab markers, progress indicators, and link highlights.
- **Tertiary (`#F59E0B`)**: Warm Amber. Reserved exclusively for contextual accents, pending statuses, deadline proximity alerts, bookmarks, and curated annotations.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8FAFC`)**: Soft Slate. Reduces ocular fatigue compared to pure `#FFFFFF` over multi-hour study sessions.
- **Surface Crisp (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure White. Dedicated to active cards, sidebars, content modules, and form inputs to create deliberate foreground separation.
- **On-Surface High Contrast (`#0F172A`)**: Slate 900. Core typographic color providing uncompromised legibility (14.5:1 contrast against `#FFFFFF`).
- **Muted Border (`#CBD5E1`)**: Slate 300. Used for definitive outlines, data table separators, and input boundaries without creating visual noise.

## Typography

The dual-script typographic strategy pairs **Inter** (Latin) with **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** (Arabic). Both share geometric harmony, open apertures, and disciplined baseline-to-x-height ratios, preventing jarring height jumps in mixed-language sentences.

### Bidirectional & Locale Guidelines
- For Latin text (`dir="ltr"`), apply negative tracking on display and headline tiers to sharpen technical density.
- For Arabic text (`dir="rtl"`), enforce `letter-spacing: 0` regardless of level to avoid breaking cursive ligatures. Increase the `line-height` by 10% to accommodate tall Arabic ascenders and descenders (e.g., Alif, Lam, Meem).
- Numerical metrics, mathematical formulations, and code blocks use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to align vertical data points in academic records.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by an 8-point base grid, functioning through a 12-column fluid grid system on desktop, an 8-column layout on tablet, and a 4-column system on mobile.

### Breakpoints & Canvas Bounds
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column grid, max-width `1440px` centered, `gutter: 1.5rem`, `margin: 2rem`. Content panels (syllabi, reading canvases) anchor to a 720px reading-measure column to preserve 60–75 characters per line.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column grid, fluid width, `gutter: 1.25rem`, `margin: 1.5rem`. Side-by-side research panes transition into expandable split sheets.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px)**: 4-column grid, fluid width, `gutter-mobile: 1rem`, `margin-mobile: 1rem`. Persistent side rails collapse to an off-canvas drawer.

### Directional Space Rules
All padding, margin, and position rules must rely strictly on logical properties (`margin-inline-start`, `padding-inline-end`) rather than physical coordinates (`left`, `right`) to guarantee natural mirroring when switching between English and Arabic.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve scholarly concentration and optimize contrast, the design system minimizes deep ambient drops and blurred drop shadows. Hierarchy is achieved using **Tonal Layering** supplemented with **Low-Contrast Structural Outlines**.

### Elevation Tiers
- **Canvas Base (Level 0)**: `#F8FAFC` (Soft Slate). Unbordered background representing the foundational desk space.
- **Surface Resting (Level 1)**: `#FFFFFF` (Crisp White). Used for modules, cards, and reading containers. Framed with a 1px solid border of `#E2E8F0` (Slate 200). Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.
- **Surface Hover / Focused (Level 2)**: Elevated modules, active course items, and interactive list selections. Uses `#FFFFFF` with a crisp border in `#CBD5E1` and an elevated shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Overlay & Popover (Level 3)**: Modals, rich reference tooltips, and contextual definition cards. Enclosed in a 1px border of `#94A3B8`, anchored by a high-definition boundary shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.1), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`.

## Shapes

The system implements a **Soft (Level 1)** shape language. The subtle radius reinforces institutional stability, precision, and architectural discipline, avoiding informal, play-oriented roundedness.

- **Base Corner Radius (`rounded`, 0.25rem / 4px)**: Applied to text input fields, selection controls, badges, chips, and small operational buttons.
- **Component Radius (`rounded-lg`, 0.5rem / 8px)**: Standard for content cards, data panels, modals, dropdown menus, and modular course containers.
- **Macro Radius (`rounded-xl`, 0.75rem / 12px)**: Restricted strictly to large modal overlays, institutional hero banners, and global viewport drawers.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Background `#1E3A8A`, text `#FFFFFF`, border `transparent`, radius `0.25rem`. On hover: `#172554`. Focus visible: outline `2px solid #0284C7` with `2px` offset.
- **Secondary**: Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#1E3A8A`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`. On hover: `#F1F5F9`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Background `transparent`, text `#0284C7`, padding `0.5rem 0.75rem`. Hover: `#E0F2FE`.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- **Informational**: Background `#E0F2FE`, text `#0369A1`, border `1px solid #BAE6FD`, radius `0.25rem`, padding `0.125rem 0.5rem`, font size `label-sm`.
- **Status Amber (Alert/Pending)**: Background `#FEF3C7`, text `#B45309`, border `1px solid #FDE68A`.
- **Academic Term / Tag**: Background `#F1F5F9`, text `#334155`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`.

### Form Controls (Inputs, Checkboxes, Radios)
- **Text Inputs**: Height `2.5rem`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #CBD5E1`, text `#0F172A`, placeholder `#94A3B8`, radius `0.25rem`, padding `0 0.75rem`. Focus: border `#0284C7`, box-shadow `0 0 0 1px #0284C7`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Size `1rem`, border `1.5px solid #64748B`, radius `2px` (checkbox) or `50%` (radio). Checked state: fill `#1E3A8A`, checkmark icon `#FFFFFF`.

### Cards & Analytical Modules
- **Academic Container**: Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, radius `0.5rem`, padding `1.5rem`. Header bar separated with a subtle horizontal rule of `1px solid #F1F5F9`.

### Academic Lists & Timetables
- **Row Architecture**: Alternating row highlights (zebra stripe) using `#F8FAFC` on even rows for tabular clarity. Row borders: `1px solid #F1F5F9`. Hover state on interactive records: background `#F1F5F9`.
- **Dual-Script Indicator**: Left border (LTR) or right border (RTL) of `3px solid #1E3A8A` denotes active or ongoing lecture items.
